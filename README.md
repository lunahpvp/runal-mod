<h1 align="center">Runal</h1>

## What is Runal?

**Runal** brings combat awareness, visual customization, progress tracking, and quality-of-life tools together in one clean ClickGUI. It is designed around ScepterRPG and gives you the information you need without cluttering your game.

## ✨ Features

Runal's modules are organized into four ClickGUI categories.

### Combat

*Stay aware and stay alive.*

- **Auto Sprint** - Automatically sprint while moving.
- **Health Indicator** - Configurable sound and title warnings when your health drops below custom low and mid thresholds.
- **Hitboxes** - Render player and entity hitboxes with custom colors and line widths.
- **Weapons / Armor / Accessory Cooldowns** - Track ability cooldowns in one panel with Percent or Seconds display modes.
- **Boss Callout** - Show an on-screen alert when a tracked raid or dungeon boss speaks.

### Visual

*Customize your HUD and view for maximum clarity.*

- **Health Bar** - Overhead health displays with Bar, Compact, and Text styles, plus damage flash and smooth interpolation.
- **Armor HUD** - Display your currently equipped armor.
- **Fullbright** - See clearly in dark areas.
- **Hide Players / Hide Armor** - Hide other players or armor on yourself and others.
- **Player Size** - Adjust the scale of your player model.
- **Teammate Track** - Highlight teammates and automatically sync members as players join, leave, get kicked, or the party disbands.

### Tracking

*Keep important information visible while you play.*

- **Session Manager** - Track playtime for your current session.
- **Performance HUD** - Display FPS, TPS, ping, and facing direction.
- **Dungeon Tracker** - View the current dungeon, room number, and rooms remaining before Parkour, Boss, or Treasure rooms.
- **Boss Defeat Counter** - Keep a running count of defeated bosses.
- **Waypoints** - Create per-dimension waypoints with optional beams.
- **Event Tracker** - Follow live server events.

### Misc

*Useful tools that make everyday gameplay smoother.*

- **Auto GG** - Automatically respond to item finds and achievement completions, with rarity filters for Legendary, Epic, and Mythical items.
- **Auto Trash** - Automatically trash unwanted shards by rarity.
- **Hotbar Swap** - Swap hotbar rows instantly with a keybind.
- **Command Binds** - Bind chat commands to keys.
- **Discord RPC** - Show your current dungeon, mining or fishing activity, and boss fights on Discord.
- **Click GUI** - Configure modules, change the accent color, manage notifications, and open the drag-and-drop HUD editor.

## ⚙️ Installation

### Requirements

1. Install the Fabric Loader for a supported Minecraft version.
2. Install the matching version of **Fabric API**.
3. Download the correct Runal `.jar` from the [latest release](https://github.com/lunahpvp/runal-mod/releases/latest).
4. Place the file in your `.minecraft/mods` folder.

Runal currently provides builds for:

- Minecraft 1.21.4
- Minecraft 1.21.11
- Minecraft 26.1.2
- Minecraft 26.2

## 🖱️ Using the ClickGUI

- Press **Right Shift** or **Left Alt** to open the menu.
- **Left-click** a module to toggle it.
- **Right-click** a module, or click its arrow, to expand its settings.
- Open **HUD Editor** from the Click GUI module settings, then drag enabled HUD elements to reposition them.
